Our Atelier

Florence · London · Paris

Born from a love of light

Aestréla was founded in 2019 on one belief: that luxury clothing should feel like a second skin — weightless, intentional, and alive. The name draws from the word for star. A quiet confidence. A presence that needs no introduction.

Each piece is conceived in London, refined in Paris, and crafted in Florence by artisans whose skill spans generations. We work exclusively with certified natural fibres and maintain a zero-waste production philosophy.

Craftsmanship

Cut, stitched, and finished by hand. No shortcuts. Garments built to be worn across decades.

Sustainability

Certified natural fibres. Low-impact dyes. Zero-waste production. Beautiful things should not cost the earth.

Bespoke

Tailoring and commissions available by appointment. Contact us to begin the conversation.
